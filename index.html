Frequently Asked Questions

Why isn't my report visible yet?

New reports are pending until a moderator approves them.

Do I need an account?

No. Anyone on campus can report or browse items.

How do I claim an item?

Find it in Browse Reports, then contact the lost & found desk with a description that proves it's yours.

What should I do after I find something?

Submit a found report and hand the item to campus security.
